  • It was 8:00 PM in Niseko, Japan,Lucas Snow woke up to a massive blizzard was dumping fresh snow. The temperature was a freezing minus eighteen degrees. Most people were inside staying warm, but I was standing at the peak strapping into my snowboard. I pushed off the ridge and dropped straight into three feet of fresh, fluffy powder. It felt like flying. Suddenly, the resort lights disappeared as I drifted into a dense forest of birch trees. Adrenaline spiked through my chest. I weaved violently between massive tree trunks in the dark. A fallen log appeared out of nowhere. With zero time to think, I popped off my back foot, launched into the air, and stomped the landing blindly into the deep snow. When I finally skidded to a halt at the bottom lift, my legs were shaking from exhaustion, but I had a massive grin on my face. That split-second thrill is the exact reason I started Powder Chase Adventures!
